Find the font in the address, and stop the seed swatch from stretching

Only a font name and an address could be typed, and the name had to be spelled
out: a stylesheet with font-face rules already says what the family is called.
The editor now reads the address and fills the name in, and an address that
points at a font file rather than a stylesheet is loaded with a font-face rule
instead of an import.

The seed colour swatch was stretched across the whole column, because the rule
that makes form inputs fill their column is more specific than the rule for the
swatch. And the palette switcher in the dashboard footer disappears once a
palette is set, which is how Homepage works but was not written down anywhere.
